Welcome to Palestine Web

Palestine is a land of profound historical and cultural significance, situated at the crossroads of Asia and Africa. This region is the cradle of ancient civilizations and a spiritual heartland for multiple faiths. It boasts a diverse landscape, ranging from the beautiful Mediterranean coastline to complex, rugged mountain ranges.

Quds

The historic capital and a focal point of faith. It is renowned for its ancient architecture and serves as the home to significant religious sites, such as the Al-Aqsa Mosque.

Khaleel

A major commercial and industrial hub in the West Bank. It is famous for traditional crafts like glassblowing and ceramics, and is home to the Ibrahimi Mosque.

Yafa

One of the oldest port cities in the world on the Mediterranean coast. It was historically a major trade center and globally famous for exporting "Jaffa oranges."

Khan Younus

A prominent city in the southern Gaza Strip. It has a long history as a resting point for merchant caravans along the ancient trade route between Egypt and the Levant.

Rafah

The southernmost border city of the Gaza Strip. It serves as a vital strategic crossing and the main gateway connecting Palestinian territories with Egypt's Sinai Peninsula.

Gaza

A major coastal city on the Mediterranean with thousands of years of continuous settlement history. It is a crucial economic, fishing, and cultural center in the region.
